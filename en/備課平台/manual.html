3. Applicable objects:

This set of digital media materials is designed forStudents in special education classes in elementary, middle and senior gradesTailor-made research and development, core applicable objects cover the following three categories and typical characteristic challenges:

  • 1. Autistic (ASD) students:Limited theory of mind, facing eye contact anxiety, weak interpretation of non-verbal expressions, high fixation, and fear of context switching resistance.
  • 2. Students with Emotional Behavioral Disorder (EBD):Prefrontal lobe impulse control is fragile, frustration tolerance is extremely low, extreme competitiveness cannot afford to lose, and instinctive defensiveness and sophistry when making mistakes.
  • 3. Students with learning disabilities (LD):Difficulty in reading, writing, and decoding, low academic self-confidence, mild and shy movements, and prone to withdrawing from peer groups.

4. Design motivation:

In the teaching practice of elementary school special education resource classes and special education classes,"Social Skills" and "Emotional Regulation"Often the most critical challenge facing schoolchildren with autism spectrum disorder (ASD) and emotional behavioral disorder (EBD). However, on-site teachers generally face the following three real dilemmas when teaching social skills:

  1. Challenge 1: A consistent, connected curriculum for the whole year Most of the commercially available special education textbooks are sporadic units or fragments of picture books, lacking a 52-week overall blueprint that runs through the adaptation to the beginning of the school year, special editions of four seasons celebrations, self-discipline in winter and summer vacations, and psychological preparation for grade promotion. Teachers prepare lessons every week like a blind man trying to figure out the elephant, and they are exhausted.
  2. Challenge 2: Making actions and consequences concrete ASD students have difficulty detecting micro-expressions and tone due to limited theory of mind; EBD students have extremely weak impulse control and often "explode first and regret later" in the classroom. Traditional paper flipcharts or static PPT are full of didactic standards and lack a "Visual Decision Tree", so children cannot see the immediate consequences of different behaviors.
  3. Challenge 3: Applying classroom skills at home and in general classrooms Children's performance in the highly structured environment of special education classes is acceptable, but when they return to the original class or family of regular classes, teachers and parents in regular classes often lack unified key reminders and behavioral support mechanisms, resulting in the rapid degradation of newly acquired skills, forming a huge gap between Putian cooperation and parent-teacher collaboration.

This teaching material team is based on"Completely solve the real pain points of the special education scene"As the core starting point, after one year of practical research and development and repeated classroom verification, we created the first comprehensive coverage in Taiwan"40-minute 5-step structured briefing", "Four Roles Differentiated Theater", "Behavioral Decision Cause and Effect Simulator", "Zihai Phonetic Color Picture and Text Study Sheet" and "Putt Integrated Family Classification Mechanism"A comprehensive digital media ecosystem for special education.

Four. Production process and R&D process

This work has undergone one year of complete research and development and on-site quenching:

  1. On-site demand exploration and pain point sorting (1-2 months):Interview special education and regular class teachers, take stock of the IEP social skills assessment of special education students, and establish a 52-week curriculum for the entire school year.
  2. Curriculum structure planning and script creation (3rd-5th month):Compose 101 life and social stories according to Carol Gray 10.2 standards, dismantling the three-step body formula for superpowers and the division of labor between the four roles.
  3. Digital system architecture and interactive program development (6th-8th month):Use pure native front-end technology (HTML5/CSS3/Vanilla JS/Web Audio/Three.js) to develop a behavioral decision-making causal simulator and a 3D floating island world, ensuring 100% offline availability.
  4. Learning single vector drawing and typesetting engineering (9th-10th month):Integrate the open-source Chinese character "Hi" in phonetic notation and regular script, draw high-resolution vector graphics cards, type the 104-page worksheet and the 106-page teacher's manual strictly according to the single-page zero-overflow specification, and use Python to inject internal jump hyperlinks into the full table of contents.
  5. Classroom empirical teaching and iterative optimization (11th-12th month):Conduct on-site empirical teaching in the learning classroom of OO elementary school, collect feedback from teachers, fine-tune the interface font level and prompt sticky notes, and anchor the formal teaching system for the entire school year.

Land, usage method (teaching activities, evaluation methods):

1. Standard 40-minute special education classroom teaching activities (five-step process guidance)

Each class is unfolded according to the TEACCH structured timeline, and the teacher's words and activities are strictly planned as follows:

Step 1: Arouse Motivation · Story Island Introduction (00-08 minutes)

[Teaching Objectives]Project life stories through the big screen, empathize with the protagonist's plight, and be aware of the physical sensations.

[Teacher’s words]"Kids watch the big screen!"Today the protagonist encounters a big challenge... If it were you, how would you feel?”

[Special education support]Voice synchronous reading assists students with weak literacy, and the emotional thermometer guides the measurement of their own emotional intensity.

Step 2: Skill Demonstration · Move Breakdown (08-16 minutes)

[Teaching Objectives]Visually dismantle the 3-step body movement formula and imprint the brain's neural circuits through body memory.

[Teacher’s words]"Stretch out your hands and follow the teacher: the first step...the second step...the third step, the movements are really standard!"”

[Special education support]Multi-sensory body movement drills, with large buttons and action diagrams for decomposed demonstrations.

Step 3: Situation Exercise · Character Theater (16-26 minutes)

[Teaching Objectives]All four students took the stage for a real-life simulation and completed positive interpersonal communication dialogues according to the role prompt cards.

[Teacher’s words]"Four superstars come on stage!" Yu, Zhen, He and Jia watched the action and lines on the big screen, Action! ”

[Special education support]Yu (fixed-point guidance), Zhen (step on the brakes and calm down), He (warm-hearted support lines), Jia (step check master of ceremonies).

Step 4: Interactive Games·Amusement Park Practice (26-36 minutes)

[Teaching Objectives]Through the behavioral decision-making causal simulator (right/wrong bifurcation), you can experience the consequences of your choices in real time.

[Teacher’s words]"The superpower actual battle begins!" If it were you, would you choose the green button or the red button? Let's see what happens!”

[Special education support]Fool-proof causal simulation, providing safe trial and error feedback and "decent steps down" repair guidance when making mistakes.

Step 5: Integration and Summary · Honorary Podium (36-40 minutes)

[Teaching Objectives]Calculate the honorary stars of the whole class, complete the worksheet and stamp it, explain the integration formula of the ordinary class and check in the family learning together.

[Teacher’s words]"Everyone has upgraded today!" Complete the worksheet and get it stamped. Remember to continue using this super power when you return to regular class!”

[Special education support]The concrete star tokens are enhanced, and the home-school task cards connect life practice.

2. Multiple Assessment Methods: Formative and Summative

  • 1. Real-world dynamic drill evaluation:In the character theater session, it is evaluated whether the students can perform target actions and speak designated social lines under visual prompts.
  • 2. Behavioral decision-making bifurcation evaluation:In the cause-and-effect simulator, it is assessed whether students can identify the different consequences of impulse and reason, and make correct bifurcation choices of behavior.
  • 3. Learn single intuition and multiple evaluation:Through the colorful picture and text worksheets of "Good Habit Circles" and "Situation Questions", you can avoid heavy writing.
  • 4. IEP individualized quantitative goal evaluation:Conduct long-term follow-up evaluation of the quantified goals of each student's Individualized Education Plan (IEP).
  • 5. Summative evaluation of token certificate:Accumulate golden stars for 3D archipelago clearance and generate a "Special Education Social Little Superman Physical Clearance Certificate" with your own name.

3. Putian integrated education and home-school standardized promotion mechanism

🏫 Regular class instructor’s 30-second fusion tips post-it note

Each lesson preparation material is provided below"Put Cooperation 30 Seconds Response Skills", ordinary class teachers only need to say the key formula (such as: "Jia, what is the brain thermometer now?"), which can arouse the neural circuits established by the children in the special education class and seamlessly generalize to the large class situation.

3. Special Education Barrier-free Design and Counseling Precautions

  • • Auditory overload protection:System-wide sound effects use Web Audio to physically synthesize soft triads and glockenspiel sounds, completely eliminating harsh buzzer screams.
  • • Visual overload protection:Passed the WCAG 2.1 AA level barrier-free contrast test, using soft off-white, light blue and grass green backgrounds to eliminate strong light stimulation.
  • • Literacy decoding scaffolding:The entire system's new characters and vocabulary are integrated with the open source "Zi Hi Phonetic Marking and Kai Style", which provides the Ministry of Education's standard phonetic symbols and supports one-click on/off.
  • • Positive non-blame trial and error mechanism:In the cause and effect simulator, even if a schoolchild chooses to act impulsively, the system will never display accusatory words, but will guide "try again" and provide repairs.
