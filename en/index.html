UDL Universal Learning Design Architecture

Multiple representation methods: Text cards, voice narration reading, traditional Chinese phonetic fonts, 3D visual islands.
Various actions and expressions: Oral dialogue, physical rehearsal and drill, learning single-choice and check-boxing, 3D level-breaking.
Multiple participation incentives: Four students’ token passbooks, real-time star sound effects, and real campus resonance.

👥 Four supports that can be freely combined

These supports are options, not profiles of particular learners or diagnoses. Teachers can combine them as needed.

Differentiated Instruction
🦖

Visual cards and sequence Choose as needed

Use three-step cards, a visual sequence and demonstrations to preview what comes next. Gradually reduce prompts when appropriate.

🦄

Reading and expression Choose as needed

Choose phonetic support, larger text, read-aloud or pointing. Speech, movement and AAC can all support participation without heavy copying.

🦁

Flexible participation Choose as needed

Observe first, practise in short parts or suggest another approach. Rotate roles and accept different ways to participate.

🚀

Sensory support and rest Choose as needed

Low-stimulation mode is on by default. Use rest and needs cards, and adjust activity pace, sound and movement to the learner.
