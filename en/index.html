Multiple representation methods: Text cards, voice narration reading, traditional Chinese phonetic fonts, 3D visual islands.
Various actions and expressions: Oral dialogue, physical rehearsal and drill, learning single-choice and check-boxing, 3D level-breaking.

👥 OO Elementary School Learning Classroom · Individualized learning scaffolding for four students

Based on the characteristics of different disabilities, tailor-made scaffolding and incentive mechanisms are provided in lesson plans, large-screen briefings and worksheets.

Differentiated Instruction
🦖

Yu Autism ASD

Preference Explicit Visual Cards and Social Stories Method. The system provides specific action formulas, a fixed 5-step structured rhythm preview and a personal token passbook to reduce anxiety.

🦄

He Learning Disability LD

Literacy and reading comprehension disadvantages. It fully adopts the open source "Zihai phonetic notation and regular script" B4 large character layout, with large-screen adaptive voice reading and image side selection.

🦁

Jia Mood disorders EBD

Easily frustrated, agitated, and emotionally impulsive. The system can switch between the 4-7-8 breathing aura and the five-sense landing method at any time. Through character theater, you can first rehearse the correct expression and then practice it.

🚀

Jun Hyperactive-impulsive ADHD

Short attention span and high energy. Stay focused with 5-step fast-paced, dynamic playground games, real-time sound streamers and 3D archipelago missions.
